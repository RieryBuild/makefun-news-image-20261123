## The 13 panels

### P1 — HERO — COP31: THE MORNING AFTER (Antalya / world) — REPORTED
The summit ended Sunday with no deal; Monday brings the verdicts. Australian PM Anthony
Albanese (AFP, Sydney, Mon 23 Nov) calls the failure a sign of "difficult geopolitical
times". UN chief António Guterres: "deeply disappointed... There may be no consensus in the
negotiating room. There is no ambiguity in the science." The Make Big Polluters Pay
alliance's release of 23 Nov: fossil-fuel accountability and climate finance are
non-negotiable; Vanuatu, Tuvalu, Timor-Leste, the UK and Spain agree to work on making
polluters pay. Unresolved issues go to the Bonn mid-year session, then COP32 in Addis
Ababa, Ethiopia, in 2027. A record 700 carbon-capture lobbyists were in the room.
Sources: AFP via NAMPA, 23 Nov 2026; globalsociety.earth, published 23 Nov 2026 00:01 GMT;
Make Big Polluters Pay release, 23 Nov 2026.

### P2 — HERO — INDIA SIX WICKETS FROM VICTORY (Wellington) — CONFIRMED
Final day, first Test, Basin Reserve. New Zealand 157-4 at lunch on Monday (Reuters,
Mon 23 Nov 00:21 UTC), still 195 behind. Overnight pair gone before lunch: Devon Conway
65, caught behind off Washington Sundar; Rachin Ravindra 37, caught by KL Rahul off
Prasidh Krishna. India need six wickets in two sessions to lead the series 1-0. India
declared 720-9 on day four (Sun 22 Nov) — the largest total by any team in a Test in
New Zealand; KL Rahul made 136. Second Test starts in Christchurch on Friday.
Source: Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 00:21 UTC.

### P3 — HERO — ASIA BRACES AS THE AI ROUT ROLLS ON (Singapore / Seoul / Taipei) — CONFIRMED
Reuters, Singapore, Mon 23 Nov: US stock futures bounce at the open (Nasdaq 100 +2-3%,
S&P 500 +~2%) while Asian markets slide after last week's wipeout for tech and chip
stocks. South Korea's KOSPI drops more than 3% in the first minutes; Taiwan's shares
are down 17% on the week, their worst week since 1990; Friday saw the Philadelphia
semiconductor index fall 10%. Seoul says it will deploy market stabilisation funds
immediately if falls continue; Taiwan's regulator tightened short-selling rules on
Saturday; China's exchanges eased margin-financing requirements. Japan's markets are
closed for a holiday; Australian stocks slightly lower.
Source: Reuters, 23 Nov 2026.

### P4 — BRITAIN: THE CASE FOR PUBLIC CONTROL (London) — REPORTED [FIX-ROUND REPLACEMENT]
The Labour government sets out the case for more public control of energy and water
utilities on Monday. Senior minister Louise Haigh addresses the CBI annual conference

government's push comes as the utilities debate — ownership, bills and investment —
returns to the front of British politics.
Source: Reuters, Mon 23 Nov 2026 ("UK government to make case for greater public
control of utilities").

### P5 — SEARCH RESUMES AT THE CIKANDE FACTORY (Indonesia) — CONFIRMED
The search for the missing resumes on Monday at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory
in Cikande, Serang, Banten. Police have recovered seven body bags and rescuers are
searching for about 50 workers still missing (Reuters, Sun 22 Nov). The fire broke out
on Saturday evening, 21 Nov; around 300 workers were on shift, 15 were rescued alive,
four of them injured. DNA identification of the recovered victims could take two to
three weeks; the cause is under investigation.
Sources: Reuters, 22 Nov 2026 (figures); search resumption Mon 23 Nov.

### P6 — CENTURION: A NEW TEST BEGINS (South Africa) — CONFIRMED (scheduled)
Second Test, South Africa v Bangladesh, begins Monday 23 Nov at SuperSport Park,
Centurion (10:00 local, 08:00 UTC). South Africa lead 1-0 after winning the opener by
nine wickets. Aiden Markram captains in place of Temba Bavuma, out with a calf strain;
Tony de Zorzi replaces him and Anrich Nortjé returns. Bangladesh lose Mushfiqur Rahim
(groin) and Hasan Mahmud (elbow). Simon Harmer keeps his place after his match-winning
turn in Johannesburg.
Sources: Cricbuzz, 23 Nov 2026.

### P9 — RUPEE FIRMS AS FLOWS POUR IN (India / markets) — REPORTED [FIX-ROUND REPLACEMENT]
The rupee closed at 92.67 to the dollar on Friday, strengthening past 93 for the first
time since April, and gaining 1.7% on the week — helped by a record $12bn of foreign
equity inflows in November, with bonds taking in another $1.2bn in the first three
weeks of the month. The benchmark 10-year bond yield ended at 7.1868%, down 12 basis
points last week. HSBC's flash PMI is due Monday at 10:30 IST (not yet out at press
time). Traders watch oil prices, the RBI's December 4 decision and the Fed's December
9 meeting.
Source: Reuters, Mon 23 Nov 2026 ("Foreign inflows lend support to rupee, bonds").

### P10 — GENEVA: CALM BEFORE GAME 1 (World Chess Championship) — CONFIRMED (scheduled) [FIX-ROUND REPLACEMENT]
Media day and a technical meeting in Geneva on Monday, ahead of Game 1 on Tuesday

Javokhir Sindarov (Uzbekistan) at the Fondation Martin Bodmer in Cologny; both are 20,
the youngest world championship match ever. At Sunday's opening ceremony the draw

Sources: FIDE / worldchess.com schedule, 23 Nov 2026; chess.com; The Hindu, 23 Nov 2026.

### P11 — SAN FRANCISCO BRACES FOR KING TIDES (California) — REPORTED [FIX-ROUND REPLACEMENT]
A National Weather Service coastal flood warning takes effect at 6 AM on Monday and
runs to 4 PM on Thursday. Monday's high tide hits 8.0 ft (MLLW) at 9:25 AM — 2.2 ft
above normal — with up to 2.8 ft of inundation in low-lying areas; tides approach the
record 8.66 ft set in January 1983. Mayor Daniel Lurie urges residents to avoid travel
in flooded areas; a storm arrives Tuesday and Wednesday.
Sources: NWS forecast, 23 Nov 2026; Bloomberg, 23 Nov 2026.

### P12 — DYMER: THE SEARCH GOES INTO DAY TWO (Ukraine) — REPORTED
Rescuers keep digging on Monday at Dymer, north of Kyiv, where a Russian drone strike
on Sunday morning collapsed part of a five-storey apartment block. Fifteen people are
missing, including a girl of 11 and a boy of 7; two bodies have been recovered and nine
people were evacuated. The strike came in a night when Russia launched 186 drones,
161 of them shot down. President Zelenskyy repeats his call for the US to release
Patriot interceptors paid for by allies.
Sources: Reuters, 22 Nov 2026 (strike, figures); search continues Mon 23 Nov.

### P13 — THE SHIP STILL BURNING OFF FINLAND (Baltic Sea) — REPORTED
A Russian cargo ship is still on fire off Finland on Monday, two days after a suspected
Ukrainian drone attack set it ablaze in the Baltic (21 Nov). An air alert sounded in
Kaliningrad hours after the attack on the Russia-bound ship.
Sources: 23 Nov 2026 news lists; AP wire, 22 Nov 2026.
